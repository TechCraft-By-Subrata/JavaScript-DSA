## File Naming Convention

All files follow the pattern: `[NUMBER] [Indian-Themed Name].md`

Examples:
- `001 Shopping List Mystery (Two Sum).md`
- `015 Monsoon Market Triplets (Indian 3Sum).md`
- `012 Monsoon Puddle Collector.md`
